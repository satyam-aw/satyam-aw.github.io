zen garden

The photographs from 2021–2023 showcase species from Santa Barbara County, documented while I was pursuing my Master's degree at UC Santa Barbara. After graduating, I relocated north. The plants listed under 2023–2026 are native species discovered along the hiking trails around the Bay Area.
