I am a Computer Science researcher and software engineer specializing at the intersection of **Spatial AI**, **Computer Vision**, **Ubiquitous Computing**, and **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**. My research focuses on building adaptive, intelligent, and context-aware systems that bridge physical environments and digital interfaces.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the **University of California, Santa Barbara**, where I conducted research at the **Four Eyes Lab** on low-latency mobile eye tracking and calibration architectures for mixed-reality environments (supported by the NSF). Prior to that, I completed my Bachelor of Technology in Electrical Engineering with a **Minor in Computer Science** from the **Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur**.

Most recently, I have worked as an engineer at **Intuit** and **Yahoo**, where I designed high-throughput data integration frameworks, state-driven mobile UI architectures, and applied machine learning tools. While my industry tenure gave me deep expertise in building production-grade, large-scale software infrastructure, my long-term goal is to return to academia. I am actively seeking **Ph.D. opportunities** where I can leverage my hybrid background to engineer the next generation of intelligent cyber-physical systems.
